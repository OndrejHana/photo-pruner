# Photo Pruning

Reviewing a shoot to choose the RAW files worth keeping.

## Language

**Source folder**:
The folder on the iPad containing the photos being reviewed.

**Review item**:
A photo presented as one choice during review. A RAW file and its JPEG companion belong to the same review item; a RAW file can also stand alone.

**RAW keeper**:
A RAW file belonging to a photo the reviewer has chosen to keep.

**Keepers folder**:
A new destination folder containing copies of the RAW keepers at the end of the pruning workflow.

**Keep decision**:
An explicit choice to include a review item's RAW files in export. A star rating alone does not imply a keep decision.

**Star rating**:
A zero-to-five rating stored with the review on the iPad. New ratings are not embedded into v1 exports and do not produce sidecars.

**Incomplete export**:
A visibly named staging folder that has not yet completed copying and integrity verification. It is never reported as a completed keepers folder.
